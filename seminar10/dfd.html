Draw an informal data flow diagram of Cherry Perfect. Draw one box for each transaction processing system (TPS) and connect the boxes with arrows. Label each arrow with the data that moves between the two systems. Systems that are not TPS don't belong on the diagram.
A TPS records and processes the routine, day-to-day transactions of the business, such as bookings, payments, employee records or equipment movements. Reporting tools and reference databases are not TPS.

Cherry Perfect Group

Cherry Perfect has grown from a small business into one of the UK’s leading providers of powered access equipment. Its services include equipment hire, sales, maintenance, training, and certification. The company also operates a related brand, Cherry People, which acts as a temporary staffing agency, supplying qualified and vetted operators to work with equipment provided by Cherry Perfect or other suppliers.

The Marketplace: Cherry Perfect and Cherry People

Cherry Perfect primarily serves building owners and contractors undertaking building maintenance, construction, and other activities requiring powered access equipment. The company rents equipment to clients through short-term and long-term contracts. It also provides maintenance and servicing contracts for clients who own their equipment.

Operating powered access equipment requires suitably trained and certified personnel. Cherry Perfect does not undertake this work directly but can provide qualified operators through its temporary staffing agency, Cherry People. The company also offers training and certification services.

Short-term equipment hires can range from four hours to three months, although most last only a few days. Long-term rentals involve extended hire arrangements and generally require less administration. Both types of rental require the company to manage customer bookings, equipment availability, rental agreements, and associated financial transactions.

Organisational Structure

The business is managed from its head office in Bolton and operates 11 depots across the UK. Four of these depots, located in Newcastle, Bolton, Nottingham, and Croydon, also serve as regional training centres and have servicing workshops and teams. In addition, the company operates 12 sub-depots in urban areas, located on sites shared with other businesses. These sub-depots primarily store smaller equipment, which is distributed by third-party providers. The company also maintains a small fleet of lorries and vans to transport equipment between depots and customer sites.

Key Information Systems

The company uses Odoo (www.odoo.com) as its main commercial system. Although the company uses only a small proportion of Odoo’s capabilities, the system handles its core financial functions, including processing financial transactions, accounting, and financial recordkeeping. The company also uses Sage HR to manage employee records and process changes to employee information, alongside Sage Payroll, which processes employee salary and wage payments.

The company uses an off-the-shelf asset management system, Asset Manager Pro, which maintains records of all equipment, including rental and internal equipment. These records include equipment identification, location, availability, and general maintenance history. Staff use the system to record and process equipment movements, including hires, returns, and maintenance updates. Detailed maintenance records associated with individual long-term leases are held separately in the long-term rental system. A reporting system, Asset Manager Reports, retrieves information from Asset Manager Pro to generate key performance indicators (KPIs), such as asset utilisation rates, to support medium-term asset management decisions.

Cherry Perfect uses a bespoke rental system to manage short-term equipment bookings, maintain rental records, and generate invoices. Customers can request equipment by telephone, email, or through the company's website. Rental staff enter customer requests into the rental system and check equipment availability using Asset Manager Pro. Once a booking is confirmed, the rental details are recorded in the rental system, and the equipment's hire status is updated in Asset Manager Pro. The rental system generates invoices and provides the invoice details to Odoo, where they are recorded for accounting purposes.

A newer version of the bespoke rental system is used to manage long-term equipment leases. This system processes lease agreements, maintains customer rental records, and records maintenance activities associated with individual leases. Asset Manager Pro maintains the central equipment register and general maintenance histories, while the newer rental system maintains detailed lease-specific maintenance records.

An existing web-based system allows Cherry Perfect's business-to-business (B2B) clients to submit requests for short-term equipment hire. Customers enter their equipment requirements and requested hire dates through the website. These requests are forwarded to the rental team, who process them using the bespoke rental system. Equipment availability is checked through Asset Manager Pro, and the equipment's hire status is updated once the booking is confirmed. Customers are then notified of their confirmed bookings. The web system provides an interface for submitting requests, while the rental system handles the booking transactions.

Cherry People uses a bespoke system called Cherry Portal, which maintains verified training records and qualifications for individual operators and issues relevant reminders. The system includes a basic portal through which individuals can submit details of their qualifications. Cherry People employees use the system to process qualification submissions, verify training records, and update operators' qualification statuses. This information is used to identify suitably qualified operators when responding to customer requests for temporary staff.

The company also uses several smaller systems, including a parts and engineering database containing technical specifications, parts information, and engineering reference materials used by workshop employees. A customer relationship management (CRM) system is used by the direct sales team to record and process customer enquiries, manage sales leads, and update sales records. In addition, employees use standard office applications and numerous spreadsheets containing various types of business data.
