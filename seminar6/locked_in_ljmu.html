A Digital Business Management escape room

Work out the door code before time runs out.

Set in Liverpool Business School, LJMU. The keypad is fictional.

Easy
10:15, Monday of Week 6. John Foster Building, Room 2.11.

Your Digital Business Management seminar started fifteen minutes ago. Dr Biały hasn't arrived, the projector shows the university logo on a loop, and your Teams message has been read by nobody. Fifteen minutes is the rule. Outside, people are crossing the courtyard towards the library. You get up to leave.

The door doesn't open.

Since last month, every seminar room in John Foster has a SmartLock keypad, on the inside too. The green exit button carries a printed sign: "Disabled during Smart Campus pilot." The keypad has nine digits and nothing else.

You reach for your phone to ask for help. The screen flashes a red battery icon and goes black. No calls, no search, no AI. Just you, the room and whatever you can work out yourself.

You have 15 minutes.

15:00Easy

Tap anything that looks useful.

The lock worked perfectly. The password system did not.
